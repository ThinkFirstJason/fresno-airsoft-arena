New players are always welcome. Bring your own gear or rent ours at check-in. Your first game is free.

01 · First day

Never played? Here's the whole morning.

Most people's first visit goes the same way. Nothing on this list takes long, and a ref walks new players through all of it.

  1. Before

    Sign online

    Fill out the waiver at home. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign.

  2. At the gate

    Check in

    Get there 15–20 minutes before start. Pay for the day, pick up rentals and fill a mag.

  3. Every gun

    Chrono

    Every gun goes over the chronograph before it goes on the field, rentals included. It takes about thirty seconds.

  4. Ref's briefing

    Safety talk

    Where the boundaries are, how to call hits, the safe zone and the minimum engagement distance.

  5. Until close

    Play

    Short rounds, then swap sides. Refill and drink water between games. In summer you'll need more than you think.

02 · The ground

Three kinds of fight on one field.

The field is open dirt with cover built in, surrounded by working orchards and vineyards. Each area plays differently, so the games don't feel the same twice.

  1. 01

    The bus

    An old city bus parked in the open. Its windows are long since shot out. Hold it and you control the middle, but everyone knows that.

  2. 02

    Plywood town

    Walls, doorways and tight corners painted red and white. It's close-quarters play: short sightlines and fast pushes, so stay on semi.

  3. 03

    Rubble line

    Broken concrete and dirt berms along the orchard edge. It's slow and patient, good for flanking or for holding an angle.

  4. —

    Boundary

    The trees outside the tape are a working farm. Stay inside it, and if a BB goes over, it stays there.

04 · Field rules

Short list. Not optional.

The refs enforce these every game. If you get them wrong, you sit the next round out.

  1. Eye protection on, past the safe zone.Full-seal goggles or a full mask. No shooting glasses. They come off only in the safe zone.
  2. Under 18: full face.Lower face protection is required for minors. Adults are strongly encouraged to wear it too.
  3. Call your hits.Hand up, shout "Hit," and walk out with your dead rag showing. Gun hits count.
  4. No blind firing.If you can't see where you're shooting, don't.
  5. Mags out, safety on, off the field.Chamber cleared before you step into the safe zone, every time.

05 · Questions

Before you drive out.

Do I need my own airsoft gun?

No. Rentals are ready at check-in, and your first game is free either way.

How old do you have to be?

The field is open to all ages. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign the waiver and must wear full face protection.

What should I wear?

Long sleeves, long pants and closed-toe boots or sneakers you don't mind getting dusty. Gloves help. A hat and sunscreen help more in summer.

Does it hurt?

Close up, a hit stings like a flicked rubber band and can leave a small welt. Layers take most of it, and minimum engagement distances keep it fair.

Can I book the field for a group or birthday?

Call (559) 307-7895 to ask about private games and group dates.

Do games run in bad weather?

Usually. Heavy rain or extreme heat can cancel a session. If the forecast looks rough, call before you drive out.
